Our Story

Born from Struggle.
Built with Purpose.

Our founder sat in those same classrooms — watching talented peers graduate without a single line of production code, no real internship, no industry mentor. The degree existed. The drive existed. The bridge to the real world didn't.

In 2025, we built that bridge. Vignan TechSolutions launched as an MSME-registered, government-recognized company with one mission: no student faces what we faced. Live internships, real-time projects, industry-aligned courses — right here in Kalaburagi, partnering with VTU CPGS.

"The struggle wasn't a setback — it was the blueprint. Every problem I faced as a student became a feature of what we built."

— Suresh Tammali, Founder & CEO
